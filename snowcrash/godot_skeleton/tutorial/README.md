# Tutorial (demo)

Five stops, each a sign with a short line of text (and optional voice + captions):
1. **Moving:** "Walk with the left stick, turn with the right. Or point and teleport."
2. **Wrist menu:** "Look at your left wrist (or press Y). This is your main menu."
3. **The bag:** "Pick up this picture with your grip button. The bag opens with everything you can do with it."
4. **Teleport:** "Press X to jump anywhere: a building's front door or straight into a room."
5. **The big picture:** "This is one building. In the full app, every drive on your PC is a building, and you can choose your world: a city, a museum, an island, a mansion."
