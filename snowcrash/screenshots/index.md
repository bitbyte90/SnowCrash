# Screenshots (11 Oct 2026, round 3)

Overview: `_contact_sheet.png` (all 57 shots).

Re-run them all: see `godot_skeleton/README.md` → "Screenshot tours" (`--tour-set=demo|museum|island|city|wrist|vr`).
